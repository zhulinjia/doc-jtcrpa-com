# Fill in and submit forms

Practice entering text, choosing options, and submitting the sample inquiry form. Its result appears only on the current page. Use the fictional details in the exercise.

## Tell AI what to submit

Open **Compile with AI** on the practice page:

<PracticeTask lesson="form" />

## Start from an empty form

AI may have filled in or submitted the form while preparing the workflow. Once the workflow card appears, click **Reset form** on the practice page, then **Manual start** in **Workbench**.

## Check every field

The page should show **Practice submission successful** with:

- Name: **Lin**.
- Topic: **Order inquiry**.
- Reply method: **On-site message**.
- **Urgent handling** selected.
- Message: **I would like an update on my order.**

Check the submission on the webpage. This task may not create a data table unless you also ask to save the submitted result.

## Use your own work form

Describe the actual text and options. Check the recipient and content before submitting in a business system. Repeating the task may submit again; ending a task does not undo a submission. If an option is uncertain, ask AI to fill in the form and wait for your confirmation before submitting.

For searching before collection, see [Search and filter](/en/tutorial/form-entry).
