# Find a tutorial for your task

Choose something similar to your work. Each tutorial starts with fictional practice data and explains how to move to your own webpage.

| What you want to produce | Suggested tutorial | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A product or book price list | [First collection](/en/guide/first-task) → [Pagination](/en/tutorial/list-pagination) | Each title matches its price, with the requested pages included. |
| A table that includes descriptions | [Lists and detail pages](/en/tutorial/list-and-detail) | Each name and its own description are on the same row. |
| A table collected from a list of URLs | [Uploaded lists](/en/tutorial/upload-table) | Each URL has a matching result and source page. |
| Search or filtered results | [Search and filter](/en/tutorial/form-entry) | The conditions are correct and the collected list is the filtered one. |
| A review summary | [Scrolling and Load more](/en/tutorial/infinite-scroll) | Later reviews are included without repeatedly collecting the first batch. |
| A completed inquiry or registration form | [Forms](/en/tutorial/fill-form) | Each entry and selection is correct, including the submitted result. |
| Emails, phone numbers, or images | [Extraction tools](/en/tools/extract-tools) | The extracted items match the webpage. |

Already have results? Continue with [Filter and format results](/en/data/tidy-results) and [Export data](/en/data/export). For collaboration, see [Feishu Bitable](/en/data/feishu).

Finish one route first. After trying it once, you can use the goal and check list to describe the next task in your own words.
